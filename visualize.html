Motion canvas

How we visualize motion with geometry

The ball is a point on a plane. Each frame we add velocity to position:

x ← x + vx   y ← y + vy

Hit a wall → reflect that component: vx ← −vx or vy ← −vy.

The orange arrow is the velocity vector (direction + speed).
