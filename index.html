Thought Visualization Lab

This site turns a complete thought into a picture you can see move.

What you will do

  1. Write any sentence. Example: the ball moves, or I walk to school.
  2. Click Visualize.
  3. The sentence appears on the canvas and moves.
  4. If the sentence talks about a ball, a ball is drawn too.

The goal is to teach visualization: a thought becomes words, words become shapes and motion on a plane.
